M8 Songs & Knobs

The song bank for the M8 LPP Emulator. Position in this list is the step button that recalls a song — the first eight sit on the eight preset steps. Open a song to edit its knobs. Changes are written to the device and a running module picks them up within a couple of seconds.

Only slots that hold a knob are listed; each page ends with a tile for the next free one. SPD is how far one detent moves the value, counted in CC steps — 1/2x is what walks a hex knob through every byte — and MIN/MAX are the ends of its travel, written in whatever the knob reads in. A knob's connection — which M8 parameter it stands for — is chosen from the catalogue on the Move, and so is the picture a group of knobs draws. Both are shown here and carried through untouched. What this page edits is the name, the CC, the send mode and the value display, plus adding, removing and moving between slots.

Absolute is the send mode the M8 reads: it learns a CC and takes the value as sent. Relative sends the movement instead and does nothing on the M8 — it is there for other gear sharing the wire.
